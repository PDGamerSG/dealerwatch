## 8. Likely problem areas (all HYPOTHESIS; themes only partly confirmed by the event page), ranked

Ranking logic: fit to the five named themes + products Azentio is actually recognised for + feasibility without customer data in a ~16 hour build.

1. AML/KYC alert triage and investigation copilot (agents + decisioning + document intelligence). Why: AMLOCK is the flagship, most awards, and "reduced false positives" and "faster investigation" are on their own pages. Data/docs: synthetic transactions and customer profiles, alert lists, sanctions lists (OFAC/EU/UN public), adverse-media articles, SAR narrative templates, KYC documents (ID, proof of address, trade licence). Build: agent that scores/explains alerts, drafts SAR narrative, cites evidence.
2. Document intelligence for onboarding / KYC / trade finance (extract, classify, validate). Data: passports, Emirates ID/Aadhaar-like sample IDs (synthetic), bank statements, invoices, letters of credit, bills of lading. Ties to "document intelligence" and trade-based AML.
3. Loan origination and credit decisioning agent (decisioning). Data: synthetic loan applications (retail/SME/corporate), income proofs, bureau-like reports, policy rulebooks, Islamic financing structures (Murabaha/Ijara terms). Build: policy-aware decision explanation, missing-document chaser, conventional vs Islamic product routing.
4. Insurance claims automation (FNOL to settlement). Data: FNOL forms, motor/health claim documents, garage estimates, surveyor reports, photos of damage, policy wordings. Build: intake classification, coverage check, fraud-score explanation, communication drafts. Matches Celent recognition and OCR/fraud features.
5. Finance operations in ERP: invoice capture, e-invoice validation, anomaly detection, natural-language ERP query (intelligent automation + customer/finance experience). Data: PDF/scanned invoices, PINT AE XML samples, GL/AP data, procurement POs. Fits the 2026 UAE e-invoicing launch and the NL ERP insights claim.
6. Customer-experience assistant for banking/insurance (customer queries, Islamic banking Q&A, product explanation, multilingual Arabic/English). Data: product FAQs, fee schedules, Shari'ah rulings, policy documents. Lower probability of being a main track because it is generic, but named in the event text.
7. Regulatory reporting and compliance knowledge assistant (RAG over regulations, report-form mapping). Data: central bank circulars, regulatory report templates. Medium-low probability.
8. Developer/delivery productivity for Azentio itself (test generation, requirement-to-config, code migration of Struts to Spring Boot, support-ticket triage via the support portal). HYPOTHESIS based on Java/Struts job post and a services business; medium-low.

Advice implied by the rules (FACT-derived): problems will come with approved synthetic data or sandboxes; no real customer data; final judging on working demo + architecture + security + scale, so prioritise a reliable end-to-end demo, an explainability/audit trail, guardrails, and clear README/architecture/AI-tool disclosure.
