# DealerWatch landing page design

A standalone, scroll-driven marketing page for DealerWatch. It lives in `landing/`, has no backend dependency, and tells the investigation story end to end: detect, gather, link, verify, record, decide. The user asked for a unique, cinematic, highly dynamic page with real 3D models, built autonomously on a new branch.

## Concept: "Read the tape"

Old market slang calls the trade record "the tape". Front-running leaves a trail on it, but the proof is spread across three systems: the dealer-desk recorder, the order blotter and KYC. The page follows one continuous ribbon of tape through dark space. Each section is a station where the tape reveals a different kind of evidence, ending at the one thing the software never does: the human decision.

Visual world: a forensic evidence board at night. Deep warm black background; the tape itself is cream paper printed with monospaced trade rows; one vermilion accent for leaks, tampering and stamps; a soft verdigris only for "verified". Film grain and a slight vignette unify the WebGL layer and the HTML layer.

Typography: Archivo variable (width axis animated by scroll velocity) for display and body, Fraunces italic for the single emphasised word in each headline, IBM Plex Mono for every number, label and tape row. Fonts are self-hosted through Fontsource packages so the page works offline.

## Sections and scroll choreography

1. **Loader.** Mono counter printing like a ticker, then a horizontal tape wipe reveals the hero.
2. **Hero.** Headline "Front-running leaves a trail on the tape." The 3D tape sweeps across the viewport with live synthetic trades printed on it. Marquee tape strip along the bottom. Two calls to action: "Follow the evidence" (scrolls) and "Open the console" (links to the app).
3. **The gap.** Three separated glass panels labelled Recorder, Blotter, KYC drift apart; scrolling pulls them into one stack. Copy on manual joins and the SEBI mandate.
4. **Detect: the sandwich.** Pinned. Particles on a time axis form a BUY cluster before the fund order slab, the price line lifts, a SELL cluster follows. Mono callouts with T-minus and T-plus timings. Detector benchmark numbers.
5. **Gather: the call.** The tape becomes a waveform ring. A transcript card shows a Hinglish leak cue with cited utterance ids; an injection line is shown struck through and ignored.
6. **Link: the path.** A constellation graph lights up in order: dealer, relative, shared phone, trading account.
7. **Verify: nine links.** Pinned horizontal scroll through nine cards; a Blender chain link snaps into place for each. End panel states the rule: full chain recommends ESCALATE, partial recommends REVIEW, pattern only recommends DISMISS.
8. **Record: the ledger.** Hash-chained blocks; one block is tampered, turns vermilion, its digest glitches, and the verifier names the sequence number.
9. **Decide: the human.** Large typographic statement "Nothing escalates itself." A bevelled seal presses a "PRIMA FACIE" stamp. Reviewer actions shown as pills.
10. **Proof.** Counting numbers from EVAL.md and the benchmark with the synthetic-data disclaimer.
11. **Fit.** Marquee of the stack and adapters: FastAPI, SQLite, NetworkX, Sarvam, Ollama, OpenAI, Anthropic, Docker.
12. **Close.** Final call to action, repository link, footer with seed and build metadata.

Global motion: Lenis smooth scroll, GSAP ScrollTrigger drives both DOM reveals and a shared progress store that the React Three Fiber scene reads every frame. Custom cursor ring with a per-section label. Magnetic buttons. Split-text headline reveals. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, which disables smoothing, pinning and the heavy post-processing.

## Architecture

- `landing/` is a Vite + React + TypeScript app. Build output is static; `npm run build` must succeed in CI with no network at runtime.
- `src/scene/` holds the R3F canvas, one `Tape` ribbon mesh with a canvas-texture of trade rows, and one component per station. Blender GLB models (`chain_link`, `slab`, `seal`) are generated by `landing/tools/blender/make_assets.py` and committed under `landing/public/models/`.
- `src/sections/` holds one component per section with its own ScrollTrigger. Sections publish normalised progress into `src/lib/scroll-store.ts`; the scene never queries the DOM.
- `src/lib/` holds Lenis setup, GSAP registration, the cursor, the loader and synthetic tape data (fictional names, scrips and figures consistent with the seeded universe).
- Post-processing: bloom, film grain, vignette, capped device pixel ratio of 1.5. Mobile uses the same page with simplified scene density and no pinning longer than one viewport.

## Acceptance

1. `npm run build` and `npm run typecheck` pass; the landing job is added to CI.
2. The page loads offline after build, with no console errors, in Chromium at 1440 wide and 390 wide.
3. Every section's scroll state is driven by one source of truth, and the scene stays above 50 fps on an Apple-silicon laptop at DPR 1.5.
4. Text content, numbers and claims match README.md and EVAL.md and keep the synthetic-data disclaimer.
5. Reduced-motion users get a readable, static-but-complete page.
